Privacy Policy

What we store

Only what you enter yourself: your name, notes, events, finance entries, records and habits. Stats about how long you use the app are kept on your device for 30 days.

Where it lives

Right now everything is stored only on your phone and encrypted there. Nothing is sent to a server. If account sync is added later, this policy will be updated first and sync will only start with your consent.

What we never do

We do not show ads, track you across other apps, sell your data or share it with anyone.

Location

Prayer times use your location only to work out the times. It is not saved or sent anywhere except to the prayer-time service to look up your zone.

App lock

If you turn on App lock, your PIN is stored only on your phone in scrambled (hashed) form. We cannot see it or reset it for you.

Your control

You can export everything at any time from Settings, or reset all your data. Removing the app from your phone removes its data.

Contact

Questions about privacy go to the developer, Adli Syahmi.
